## Proposed labor-accountability record

Before any paid work begins, the fiscal host should approve all of the
following in writing:

- a monthly or hourly rate for Jakob Hedrich;
- a maximum number of hours or a milestone-based statement of work;
- deliverables and acceptance criteria for each payment period;
- contemporaneous time records or milestone completion records;
- invoices or payment requests reviewed by someone other than the payee;
- conflict disclosure and recusal/independent-approval procedure;
- treatment of equipment, compute, subscriptions, contractors, and shared
  infrastructure;
- a clear rule that hosted funds cover only the public-interest scope.

The proposed labor plan is six monthly work periods aligned to
[MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md). It is not an authorization to pay and contains
no approved rate.

## Compensation controls

Any compensation to Jakob Hedrich is a related-party transaction and requires
written fiscal-host approval, a stated monthly or milestone rate, a defined
scope, work records, acceptance evidence, payment schedule, and recusal or
independent approval as required by the host. Hosted funds must not be used for
excluded HAL SUPREME, commercial, media, branding, or private-asset work.

No number is invented here because an unapproved amount would be misleading.
