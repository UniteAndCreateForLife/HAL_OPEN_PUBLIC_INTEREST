# Contributing

1. Explain the public-interest problem and the independent usefulness of the
   proposed change.
2. Keep credentials, private assets, personal data, and product-specific
   integrations out of the repository.
3. Add deterministic tests for policy and provenance behavior.
4. Document network, filesystem, subprocess, and dependency behavior.
5. Include before/after evidence for changes that affect output or security.
6. Preserve the Apache-2.0 notices and identify third-party material.
7. Do not claim hosted approval, public release, or capability maturity without
   a dated evidence record.
