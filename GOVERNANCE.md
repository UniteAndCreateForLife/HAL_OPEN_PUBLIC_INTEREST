# Governance

## Authority

The public-interest repository is governed by its published scope, issue and
review history, release receipts, and the applicable fiscal host agreement.
HAL SUPREME's private EventStore and WorkGraph are not authorities here.

## Required review

Each release should include:

1. an inventory of changed files and dependencies;
2. tests run with exact command and result;
3. a security/privacy review for data and network behavior;
4. an independent review when the change affects policy or trust boundaries;
5. a statement of unresolved uncertainty.

## Funding boundary

Hosted funds may support only approved public-interest scope. They must not
subsidize private product development, private media production, personal
branding, advertising, or unrelated HAL SUPREME work.

## Decision records

Material scope, license, compensation, conflict, security, or data decisions
must be recorded in a dated decision document before release.
