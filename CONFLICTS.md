# Conflict-of-interest disclosure

This draft is prepared with the understanding that Jakob Hedrich controls or
operates the broader HAL SUPREME / UniteAndCreateForLife work. That broader
work may include commercial, product, media, personal-brand, or private
creative activity.

The same developer identity, workstation, storage, compute, subscriptions,
and source history may be used across private and public-interest work unless
they are deliberately segregated. This is a material conflict and operational
risk, not a footnote. The release plan therefore requires separate
directories, separate work records, scope-limited labor reporting, and review
of shared resource allocation before any hosted expenditure.

The proposed public-interest repository must remain separately scoped and
accounted for. Hosted funds must not subsidize excluded private activities.
That includes advertising, monetization, commercial media, personal branding,
private voice/avatar assets, proprietary integrations, credentials, unrelated
automation, and unreleased product work. Shared compute or subscriptions must
be allocated by recorded work units or excluded from reimbursement when that
cannot be measured fairly.
Reusable outputs created within the approved public-interest scope remain
available under the published open license, subject to third-party rights.

The final disclosure, related-party procedure, compensation terms, fiscal-host
approval process, and any recusal requirements must be reviewed and accepted
by the fiscal host before funding or paid work begins. This file is a draft
disclosure, not legal advice or a substitute for the host's forms.
