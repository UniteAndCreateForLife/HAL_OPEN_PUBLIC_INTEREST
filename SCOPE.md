# Scope

## In scope

- local-first route and data-locality contracts;
- inspectable permission boundaries;
- portable provenance and audit formats;
- reproducible offline evaluation fixtures;
- documentation and small reference implementations;
- privacy-preserving, user-controlled knowledge-store interfaces;
- threat models and adversarial tests for the above.

## Explicitly out of scope

- HAL SUPREME's private orchestration and product code;
- commercial media production, monetization, advertising, or publishing;
- personal branding and private creative assets;
- proprietary voice, avatar, face, or identity data;
- credentials, browser profiles, tokens, OAuth state, or private integrations;
- unreleased product work;
- paid provider accounts or autonomous spending;
- public YouTube/OBS control;
- claims that a system is conscious or sentient.

## Independence test

Every proposed deliverable must remain useful if HAL SUPREME as a commercial
or media product never existed. If it fails that test, it belongs in the
private integration repository or requires an explicit governance decision.
