## Proposed labor-accountability record

Before any paid work begins, the fiscal host should approve all of the
following in writing:

- a monthly or hourly rate for Jakob Hedrich;
- a maximum number of hours or a milestone-based statement of work;
- deliverables and acceptance criteria for each payment period;
- contemporaneous time records or milestone completion records;
- invoices or payment requests reviewed by someone other than the payee;
- conflict disclosure and recusal/independent-approval procedure;
- treatment of equipment, compute, subscriptions, contractors, and shared
  infrastructure;
- a clear rule that hosted funds cover only the public-interest scope.

The proposed labor basis is **$60/hour for documented work performed directly
within the approved hosted scope**, capped at **20 hours per week for 26 weeks**,
for a maximum six-month compensation of **$31,200**. Compensable work includes
engineering, testing, documentation, security/reproducibility work, project
administration, required reporting, and stakeholder coordination directly
attributable to the hosted project. Compensation excludes unrelated HAL
SUPREME, media, advertising, commercial, or personal activity.

This proposed labor plan is organized across six monthly work periods aligned
to [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md). It is not an authorization to pay: the rate,
scope, cap, records, and payment process remain subject to written fiscal-host
review and approval.
